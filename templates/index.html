Frequently Asked Questions

Need help understanding AI readability?

What is AI Readability and why does it matter? ▲

AI search tools like Perplexity, ChatGPT, and Google AI Overviews read web pages directly to summarize answers for users. If your website lacks semantic HTML, blocks crawler bots, or omits JSON-LD schema, AI models may ignore or misrepresent your business.

How do audit credits work? ▲

Every new user account receives 5 complimentary credits upon registration. Each web address audit consumes 1 credit.

How do I apply the fixes provided by the audit? ▲

Simply copy the code snippets generated in your audit results and paste them into your website’s HTML template, CMS, or root server directory.
